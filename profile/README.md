## 🧠 Overview

**Autodesk Revit** is a professional BIM and building design platform developed for architects, structural engineers, MEP specialists, contractors, and construction planners who require data-driven modeling and coordinated project development tools.

The software improves architectural workflows through intelligent building modeling, documentation control, structural planning, parametric design, collaboration features, quantity analysis, and construction-ready drafting within a centralized environment.

It is widely used for residential planning, commercial buildings, infrastructure projects, MEP coordination, structural layouts, interior planning, digital construction management, and multidisciplinary BIM development.

Built for modern Windows-based engineering and architecture workflows, Autodesk Revit enables precise project coordination from concept design to technical documentation.
